Quality Control

Quality is in the construction, the finish and the final check.

Attention to product requirements continues through production and packing, helping reduce avoidable mistakes before an order reaches the customer.

Our Quality Approach

Important details receive attention at every stage.

The right product is not only about appearance. Construction, dimensions, finishing and packing all contribute to the final result.

Construction

Attention to the required knitted or braided construction and the product's intended application.

Specifications

Understanding customer requirements for colour, width or diameter, thickness, length and other agreed details.

Finishing

Careful attention to the product's surface, appearance and finishing requirements.

Final Packing Inspection

Personal attention to packing and final inspection so incorrect or defective products are less likely to reach customers.

A Personal Commitment

Final packing is not treated as an afterthought. It is an important opportunity to check the product before it leaves for the customer.
