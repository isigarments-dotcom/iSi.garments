Our Products

Knitted and braided products, made around your specifications.

We work with garment and related product requirements across a focused range of knitted trims and cords. Colours, dimensions, constructions and finishing can be discussed for your order.

01 / Knitted Trim

Knitted Rib

Premium knitted rib trims designed for comfort, flexibility and a clean garment finish. Suitable for hoodie cuffs and waistbands, sweatshirts, jackets, tracksuits, collars and sleeve openings. Customizable colours, widths, structures and specifications.

02 / Knitted Cord

Knitted Drawcord

Quality knitted drawcords with attention to texture, flexibility and finishing. Suitable for hoodies, joggers, trousers, shorts, sportswear and casualwear. Available for discussion in different colours, thicknesses, lengths and finishes.

03 / Braided Cord

Braided Drawcord

Premium braided drawcords designed for strength, durability and a refined appearance. Suitable for hoodies, sweatshirts, joggers, trousers, jackets and sportswear. Discuss colour, diameter, braiding pattern and length requirements.

04 / Braided Cord

Braided Paracord

Durable braided cords with attention to uniform construction and appearance. Suitable for garment accessories, utility and decorative uses, and outdoor or general-purpose applications. Colour, diameter, construction and length can be specified.

05 / Knitted Decorative Cord

Knitted Makroma Dori

Knitted decorative cord with distinctive texture and consistent construction. Suitable for garment decoration, decorative textile work, craft and macrame, and customized textile accessories. Discuss colour, thickness and length requirements.
