Manufacturing

Practical manufacturing knowledge, from machine operation to final packing.

Our approach is built around understanding the construction, machinery and finishing behind knitted and braided textile products—not simply running equipment.

Production Experience

Experience across connected textile processes.

The work behind a finished cord or trim involves material preparation, machine settings, construction, finishing and inspection.

01 / Knitting

Knitted structures and trims

Hands-on experience with hand-flat knitting, circular knitting, single-bed hand knitting, drum Jacquard work, linking and knitted dori production. This practical background informs our understanding of knitted rib, knitted drawcord and decorative knitted cords.

02 / Braiding

Braided cord production

Direct operation of braiding machines, with attention to the construction, appearance and consistency of braided drawcords and paracord.

03 / Winding

Cone winding and preparation

Operation of cone winding equipment as part of the production workflow, with practical attention to material handling and preparation.

04 / Tipping

Tipping and finishing

Operation of tipping equipment and attention to the finished cord end and overall presentation according to the agreed product requirement.

05 / Dyeing

Dyeing mill experience

Practical work across dyeing-machine operation, colour room, laboratory, shift in-charge and assistant master responsibilities provides an understanding of colour and textile processing operations.

06 / Garment finishing

Finishing and handling

Experience includes cutting and steam pressing waistcoat work, plus broader textile finishing work. Stitching is not claimed as an area of expertise.

Machine Troubleshooting

Understanding the mechanism behind the machine.

Electronic and mechanical troubleshooting has been learned independently through hands-on experience, without claiming formal engineering qualifications.

  • Diagnosing electronic faults in knitting machines
  • Tracing mechanical problems through the machine mechanism
  • Understanding the function of individual machine parts
  • Making practical replacement parts from available scrap when an original component cannot be sourced

Practical experience, close to every stage of production.

Personal Involvement

Production does not end when the machine stops.

I remain personally involved in operating the braiding, winding and tipping machines, and in checking the packed product before it leaves.

Final packing inspection is handled personally to reduce the chance of an incorrect or defective product reaching the customer. Digital ledger and accounting records are also maintained as part of day-to-day working discipline.
